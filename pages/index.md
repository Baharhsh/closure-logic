This site explores how closures and fasteners are used across adaptive, medical, functional, and protective clothing. The project brings together selected commercial products and relevant patents in a structured digital collection to examine how closure design relates to garment function and user needs.

Closures can influence dressing independence, dexterity demands, medical access, caregiver assistance, comfort, safety, and adjustability. By comparing existing examples, this project seeks to identify common closure strategies and areas where current designs may not adequately address user needs.
